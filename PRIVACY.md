# Privacy Policy — Schibsted Popup Remover

**Last updated: 2026-06-04**

Schibsted Popup Remover does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal
data.

- The extension runs entirely on your device.
- It does not send any information to the developer or to any third party.
- The only data it stores is a single on/off preference, saved locally in your
  browser via `chrome.storage.local`. This never leaves your device.
- It reads and modifies the content of web pages you visit solely to detect and
  remove cookie-consent popup overlays. It does not read, log, or transmit page
  content, browsing history, or form data.

## Permissions

- **storage** — to remember whether the extension is turned on or off.
- **Host access (all sites)** — required so the content script can detect and
  remove consent popups on whatever site shows one.
